What did Hillel tell the convert?

1Gemara Shabbos 31a דעלך סני לחברך לא תעביד 2Rashi Rashi, Shabbos 31a ריעך וריע אביך אל תעזוב 3Gemara Shabbos 31a, the other converts שוב מעשה בנכרי אחד

A non-Jew came to Shammai and asked to be converted on condition that he be taught the whole Torah while standing on one foot. Shammai pushed him away. He came to Hillel, who converted him and told him1:

דעלך סני לחברך לא תעביד, זו היא כל התורה כולה, ואידך פירושה הוא, זיל גמור

“What is hateful to you, do not do to your fellow. That is the whole Torah; the rest is its explanation. Go and learn it.”

Rashi reads “your fellow” as Hashem as well, from Mishlei 27:10: “Your friend and your father’s friend, do not forsake”2.

The same amud tells two more stories of converts Shammai turned away and Hillel accepted3.
