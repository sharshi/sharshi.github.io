Lech Lecha · Reviʼi · Bereishis 14:18–20

יחוּמַלְכִּי־צֶדֶק מֶלֶךְ שָׁלֵם הוֹצִיא לֶחֶם וָיָיִן וְהוּא כֹהֵן לְאֵל עֶלְיוֹן

And Malki-Tzedek, king of Shalem, brought out bread and wine; he was a kohen to the Most High.

יטוַיְבָרְכֵהוּ וַיֹּאמַר בָּרוּךְ אַבְרָם לְאֵל עֶלְיוֹן קֹנֵה שָׁמַיִם וָאָרֶץ

He blessed him and said: Blessed is Avram to the Most High, Owner of heaven and earth.

כוּבָרוּךְ אֵל עֶלְיוֹן אֲשֶׁר מִגֵּן צָרֶיךָ בְּיָדֶךָ וַיִּתֶּן לוֹ מַעֲשֵׂר מִכֹּל

And blessed is the Most High, who delivered your enemies into your hand. And he gave him a tenth of everything.

Then: Tehillim 55–59

Question 1 of 3

What did Avram give Malki-Tzedek?

Right. Pasuk 20: וַיִּתֶּן לוֹ מַעֲשֵׂר מִכֹּל. Rashi brings that Malki-Tzedek is Shem, son of Noach.
