Gemara · Shabbos 31a

דעלך סני לחברך לא תעביד, זו היא כל התורה כולה

Ana Avda · Shabbos 31a
Explain this Who was Shammai? What does Rashi add?

A non-Jew asks Shammai to convert him on condition he is taught the whole Torah while standing on one foot. Shammai pushes him away. Hillel converts him and gives him this one line, then sends him off to learn the rest: זיל גמור, “go and learn it.”

Rashi reads “your fellow” as Hashem as well, from Mishlei 27:10.
