Explain this

A non-Jew asks Shammai to convert him on condition he is taught the whole Torah while standing on one foot. Shammai pushes him away. Hillel converts him and gives him this one line, then sends him off to learn the rest.

Who was Shammai?

Shammai was Hillel’s colleague, the last of the zugos, the pairs who led the generations before the Tannaim. Their students became Beis Shammai and Beis Hillel, whose disputes run through the Mishnah. [Ana Avda cites its sources here.]
