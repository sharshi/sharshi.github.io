Sees: 1 · Shabbos 31a 2 · Rashi
How does Rashi’s reading change what Hillel said?

In pane 1, Hillel tells the convert not to do to his fellow what is hateful to him. Rashi, in pane 2, reads “your fellow” as Hashem too, from Mishlei 27:10, so the line covers mitzvos between a person and Hashem as well as between people.
