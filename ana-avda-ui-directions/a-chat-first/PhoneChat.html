What did Hillel tell the convert?

In Shabbos 31a, a non-Jew asks to learn the whole Torah on one foot. Hillel tells him:

דעלך סני לחברך לא תעביד, זו היא כל התורה כולה

(“What is hateful to you, do not do to your fellow. That is the whole Torah.”)
